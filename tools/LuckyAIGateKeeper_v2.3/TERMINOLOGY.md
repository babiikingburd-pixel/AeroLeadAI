# Lucky AI Solutions Terminology

## "Gatekeeper"
Scientific/engineering oversight layer that tests assumptions and distinguishes
reality from simulation.

## "The Commander"
AI orchestration/supervision layer for agents, tools, workflows, authorization,
verification, and escalation.

## "We can't"
A constraint that requires investigation, not an automatic conclusion.

## "Longer route"
An alternate engineering path involving intermediate technologies,
infrastructure, resources, experiments, or scale.

## "Do more with less"
Optimize useful capability per unit of compute, data, money, time, and
infrastructure.

## "Future technology"
A label requiring decomposition. Determine whether the underlying primitives
already exist.

## "Simulation"
A model or test representing a real-world capability. It is not proof that the
real-world capability exists.

## "Reality gate"
The point where a capability is promoted only after real-world dependencies,
verification, permissions, and measurable tests pass.
