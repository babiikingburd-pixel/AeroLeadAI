# APEX 14.0 MAX — Predictive Property Intelligence

Cumulative from all prior releases. The property is the central object; storms, permits, weather, imagery, trees and utilities are inputs.

## Predictive layers
1. Property condition state
2. Maintenance-needs prediction
3. Safety/necessity prediction
4. Opportunity prediction
5. Time-to-maintenance prediction
6. Confidence/evidence quality
7. Continuous re-evaluation

## Prediction horizons
- 0–6 months
- 6–12 months
- 12–24 months
- 24+ months

## Evidence chain
Observation -> finding -> condition -> trend -> predicted need -> urgency -> opportunity -> score.

Predictions are screening estimates, not professional inspections, guarantees, insurance coverage decisions, or engineering/arborist/electrical certifications.
