# AeroLeadAI 2051 Technology Map

## Already available in 2026
- Multimodal AI
- Computer vision
- Satellite imagery
- Historical imagery
- LiDAR
- SAR
- Thermal imaging
- Drone inspection
- AI agents
- Digital twins
- Predictive modeling
- Edge AI
- Spatial/AR computing

## Underused opportunities
1. Temporal computer vision: measure how properties change over years.
2. Evidence fusion: combine imagery, weather, permits, property records and economics.
3. Digital twins: make the property, not the lead, the primary object.
4. Closed-loop learning: record actual contractor outcomes and use them to improve predictions.
5. Change detection: prioritize newly changed properties instead of repeatedly scoring static records.
6. 3D property modeling: calculate roof geometry, pitch, surface area and exposure.
7. Predictive storm damage: combine storm physics with property characteristics.
8. Agent orchestration: let specialized AI agents investigate different evidence lanes.

## Technologies likely to mature between now and 2051
- Much denser Earth-observation constellations
- Autonomous drone inspection
- Continuous building sensing
- Physical-world foundation models
- Advanced edge/neuromorphic/photonic computing
- High-fidelity spatial interfaces
- Autonomous field robotics
- More continuous digital twins of physical assets

These are forward-looking possibilities, not guaranteed predictions.
