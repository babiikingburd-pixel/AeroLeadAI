# ELITE AI 2076 Architecture

## 1. Observation Layer

The system continuously receives evidence from:

- Earth observation
- aerial imagery
- drones
- LiDAR
- SAR
- thermal
- weather/radar
- permits
- property records
- connected building sensors
- contractor observations

## 2. Property Digital Twin

Every property becomes a persistent stateful object.

The twin remembers:

- geometry
- materials
- construction history
- imagery
- storms
- permits
- repairs
- condition
- predictions
- interventions
- outcomes

## 3. Temporal World Model

The AI does not merely classify images.

It models change.

Question:

> What changed, how quickly did it change, and what is likely to change next?

## 4. Physical-World Reasoning

Future foundation models should combine:

- visual evidence
- spatial geometry
- temporal history
- weather physics
- construction knowledge
- economic information

## 5. Prediction

Predict:

- damage
- deterioration
- maintenance
- replacement
- insurance events
- economic opportunity
- timing

## 6. Action

Dial-A-Trade receives qualified opportunities and matches:

- geography
- trade
- certifications
- capacity
- performance
- estimated job type
- customer requirements

## 7. Outcome Learning

Every real-world result becomes evidence.

Prediction -> action -> result -> learning -> better prediction.

This is the critical flywheel.
